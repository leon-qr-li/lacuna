# 间隙: open a silent stretch while editing (2026-10-30)

Leon (2026-10-30) needed to leave room in an already finished episode: a stretch where nobody talks, to be
filled with music later. Design page: https://claude.ai/artifact/7EhzPD61cUnheGGVpH1bSM. He picked
**direction A, gaps only** (no segments in the UI) and these details:

| Question | Pick |
|---|---|
| Default length of a new gap | 15 s (chips 5 / 10 / 15 / 30, or any 1–300 s) |
| With music in it | the gap keeps its length; the music fades in and out inside it |
| Empty gaps at export | ask first (「还有间隙没配乐」), then export them as silence |
| Where it goes | snapped to the nearest pause between sentences (within 4 s) |
| How to add | right-click the editing timeline away from a selection, 「＋」 between two transcript lines, or G (at the playhead) |

## How it works
* `<episode>/gaps.json` holds the gaps (`lacuna_app/gaps.py`), each anchored in base samples like an interlude,
  so later voice edits move it with its sentence. The voice edit (edits, EDL, music.json) is never written by
  adding, changing or removing a gap; removing the last gap gives back the same mix key and export key as before.
* The time opens in the final mix (`music.layout`): an empty gap is an insert of silence after any music at the
  same point. 「给间隙自动配乐」 (`music_assist.fill_gaps`, a suggestion heard before it is accepted) puts one
  休息间隙 piece into each empty gap as an interlude with `gap_id`; it plays from the gap's start for at most the
  gap's length (fade in = ramp_s capped at a quarter of the gap, fade out 3 s capped at a third). Moving that
  interlude elsewhere detaches it; removing the gap removes its music.
* The editing timeline speaks voice-cut time, so a gap shows there as a mark at its point; it is heard with
  「带配乐听」 (offered as soon as a gap exists), on 配乐 and in the export. Show-notes timestamps shift with it
  (publish.music_info uses the same layout).
