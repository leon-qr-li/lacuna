# 自动铺底 (auto background music) — 2026-10-30

Leon picked direction A of the design page (https://claude.ai/artifact/R5AoVL7HeRWCP2Svnc3ER1): 一键随机铺满.

- On 配乐, the 「自动铺底」 card lists the songs this episode can use (what each is already used as), how much free
  talk there is (no intro / outro / gap / interlude music, nor its background) and how long the ticked songs are.
- Ticked by default: the songs whose use is 「聊天背景音」 (kind `bed`) and are not used yet.
- 「随机铺满空档」 makes a suggestion (the usual draft: heard first, applied with 采用). Asking again
  (「再随机一次」) replaces the earlier 自动铺底 beds with a new random order; other music is never touched.
- A song shorter than its stretch: the next song follows, 2 s crossfade. A stretch ends before the song: it fades
  out over the last 3 s. When the ticked songs run out, they go round again in a new order.
- Free stretches shorter than 20 s get no bed (Leon). After a song, when less than 6 s of the stretch is left, no
  further song starts.
- The beds are ordinary bed placements (`source: "autobed"`), at the bed default level and evened out
  (`2026-10-30-even-background.md`); Leon can change or remove each one like any other.

Code: `lacuna_app/autobed.py`, `lacuna_app/routes/autobed.py`, `web/src/features/autobed/`; tests
`tests/app/test_autobed.py`, `web/e2e/autobed.spec.ts`.
