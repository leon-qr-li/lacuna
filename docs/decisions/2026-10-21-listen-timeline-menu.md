# 整集试听 timeline: a right-click-style menu at the selection

- Date: 2026-10-21
- Status: CONFIRMED（Leon, 2026-10-20 21:36Z「就用这个方案」）
- Supersedes / changes: listen-workbench

No minimap, no 听选中/取消 bar, no time-range text. Legend in the title row on the right; track height never
changes. After a drag (or right-click inside the selection) a small vertical menu opens at the selection's right
edge, centred between the tracks: 删掉这段 (Ctrl X) / 只静音 <host>…; it flips left without room, Esc or a
click elsewhere closes it.
