# Plans: 免费体验 / 基础版 / 专业版, same features, different amounts

Date: 2026-10-21. Decided by Leon (batch 3 decision pack, questions 2–5 and 11, option A each; 03:01Z).
Supersedes / changes: — (hour quota enforcement stays in decision `ai-included-hour-quota`, batch 5).

## Decision
| | 免费体验 / Free trial | 基础版 / Basic | 专业版 / Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audio | 1 h, once | 4 h a month | 12 h a month |
| Shows | 1 | 1 | 5 |
| Collaborators per show | 0 | 2 | unlimited |

- Every plan has every feature; max 4 hosts on every plan (decision `four-hosts-max`). The trial gets a
  sample episode and no audio watermark.
- A plan belongs to an account; a show uses the plan of the account that made it (its collaborators don't
  spend their own). Shows and collaborators are enforced now (lacuna_app/plans.py, routes/spaces.py);
  audio hours are counted and enforced in batch 5 (until then 套餐与用量 shows "—, 计时稍后开放").
- Leon and Pei (accounts that existed before batch 3) are on Pro for free (`plan_free`). New invitees
  default to 基础版; the inviter can pick the plan in 设置 → 成员与邀请, and the site owner / site admins
  change anyone's plan there.
- 设置 → 套餐与用量: your plan, shows used, collaborators per show, all plans side by side. No payment
  button; 「想换套餐，请回复邀请邮件联系我们」.

## Why
Charging by usage, not features, keeps the product whole for everyone and ties price to our real cost
(AI + storage per audio hour). A one-time trial hour lets people try without a monthly giveaway.
