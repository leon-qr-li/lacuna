# The company is Canadian (British Columbia), owned by Leon alone

- Date: 2026-10-25
- Status: Leon's decision (thread 第六批, 05:25Z: 「我在加拿大开」; 05:26Z 「现在这公司是我一个人的，nobody else」)
- Supersedes / changes: the open question "company in Canada or US" on the pre-beta list

Leon lives in Vancouver as a permanent resident. Claude recommended a BC corporation: one tax system for a Canadian
resident (a US LLC is double-taxed in Canada; a US C-corp managed from Canada may be resident in both), the small-business
rate (~11 % on the first CAD 500k), possible SR&ED credits, and no obstacle to selling to US users (Paddle / Stripe
support Canadian companies; a Canadian company can register a US DMCA agent). A Delaware parent can be added later if
US venture money is ever raised.

- The company is incorporated in British Columbia before LACUNA charges anyone. Leon talks to an accountant (timing,
  structure) and a lawyer (ToS, privacy, DMCA agent) first.
- Leon is the only owner, no partners for now. Pei is not a partner (Pei still co-hosts the show and helps test).
- The terms and privacy policy name a BC company, BC law and Vancouver courts; the entity's name and address are filled
  once it is registered.
