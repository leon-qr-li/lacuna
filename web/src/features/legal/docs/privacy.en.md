# LACUNA Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-25 · Effective 2026-10-25

> This is the version for the LACUNA beta. Before public launch it will be reviewed by a lawyer and updated; if it changes in a material way, we will tell you in advance and ask you to confirm again.

## The short version

- **Your recordings stay in our own storage** (servers and cloud storage in the United States, encrypted) and are **never sent to any AI service**. Speech recognition runs on our own servers.
- To suggest edits, we send only **text** (the transcript) and **numbers** (timings, loudness and the like) to an AI model provider; by contract it may not use them to train models.
- We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or ad tracking.
- You can ask to see, export or delete your data at any time.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account information**: your email, the name you give, your password (only as an encrypted hash; we never see it), your two-step secret, interface language, plan and quota.
- **Your content**: the recordings you upload (one track per person), music and images; the transcripts, edit history, learned editing habits, show notes, posters and exports made from them; show details (show name, hosts' names and so on).
- **Collaboration information**: whom you invited, who invited you, and your role in each show.
- **Sign-in and security information**: sign-in times, IP address, browser and system type (to spot unusual sign-ins and warn you when a new device signs in), and the devices you are signed in on.
- **Usage records**: what you do in the app (for example importing, exporting, accepting or restoring an edit), recorded only as numbers and categories, with no transcript text, file names or emails, linked to an internal ID. We use them to improve the product, count quota and find problems. For support and account security, the site owner can view an account's usage records in the admin (again without any content), and every such look is itself recorded.
- **What you send us**: feedback (including screenshots you choose to attach), access requests, extra-hours requests and emails.
- **Cookies and browser storage**: see section 9.

We don't collect payment information (the beta is free). When payments are added, card details will be handled by the payment provider; we won't store card numbers.

## 3. How we use it

- To provide the Service: storing and playing your recordings, transcribing, suggesting edits, exporting, and letting you edit together with your collaborators;
- For accounts and security: sign-in, two-step verification, detecting and stopping abuse, new-device alerts;
- To contact you: verification codes, invites, quota and service notices, replies to your feedback;
- To improve the product: using aggregated usage data to see whether features work and how accurate the AI editing is (for example the share of edits people restore);
- To meet legal obligations and handle copyright complaints and lawful requests.

We do **not** use your recordings or transcripts to train AI models, use your data for advertising, or sell it.

## 4. How AI processes your content

- **Your recordings never leave our storage.** Speech recognition (turning sound into text) runs on our own servers.
- To suggest edits, write show notes and answer your questions in the app, we send **transcript text and numeric features** (timings, pause lengths, loudness and the like) through an API to an AI model provider in the United States. Under our commercial agreement it **may not use this content to train models** and keeps it only for the limited time needed to process requests and prevent abuse.
- The AI only makes suggestions; you decide which edits to keep or restore.

## 5. Legal bases (for users in the EEA, the UK and similar places)

- **Contract**: to provide the Service you signed up for (account, storage, transcription, editing, export);
- **Legitimate interests**: security, preventing abuse and improving the product, balanced so that your rights are not harmed;
- **Consent**: for example when you let us quote your feedback; you can withdraw it at any time;
- **Legal obligation**: for example handling copyright complaints and lawful requests.

## 6. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We share it only in these cases, and only what is needed:

- **Your collaborators**: members of a show you share can see, hear and edit its content and see your name and email;
- **Infrastructure providers working for us** (by contract they may process data only for us and for nothing else):
  - cloud servers (San Francisco data centre, USA): run the Service and hold your data;
  - cloud storage and network security (stored in Western North America): hold recordings you haven't opened recently and the daily encrypted backups, and protect against attacks and bots;
  - email delivery: verification codes, invites and notices;
  - AI model service (USA): receives only text and numbers, see section 4;
- **Legal requirements**: when the law, a court or a government lawfully requires it, or to protect users, the public or our rights and safety;
- **Business changes**: if LACUNA is merged, acquired or transferred, data moves with it, the new owner stays bound by this policy, and we will tell you in advance.

To get the list of our providers by name, contact us as in section 13.

If you use the Service from outside the US (for example Europe, the UK, Canada or Asia), your data is transferred to and processed in the US. We operate from Canada and store data in the US, so it may be accessible to US courts, law-enforcement and national-security authorities under US law. ## 8. How long we keep it

- **Account information**: as long as your account exists.
- **Original track recordings**: deleted automatically when an episode has not been used (opened, edited or exported) for 12 months, with an email to the show's creator 30 days before; **this rule is paused during the beta**, so recordings are kept until you delete them. Recordings of episodes not opened for 14 days move to our encrypted cloud storage and come back automatically when you open the episode.
- **Exports**: once an episode has a newer export, the older export's audio files are deleted after 7 days (the export record stays and can be made again at any time).
- **Transcripts, edit history and show notes**: until you delete the show or your account.
- **Deleted shows or accounts**: gone from the Service at once and **erased completely within 60 days**; encrypted backups rotate on a schedule of daily copies for 30 days and monthly copies for 12 months, then expire automatically.
- **Usage records**: 13 months; once an account is deleted they can no longer be linked to you.
- **Feedback and emails**: kept after they are handled so we can follow up; you can ask us to delete them.

## 9. Cookies and browser storage

We only use what the Service needs to work:

- **Sign-in cookie**: keeps you signed in;
- **Language cookie**: remembers your interface language;
- **Browser local storage**: remembers interface preferences (for example dark / light theme, open panels);
- **Security checks**: our network-security provider may set necessary cookies to check that visitors are human and to block attacks.

We use no analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, so no separate consent is needed.

## 10. Security

- Every connection is encrypted with HTTPS; data is encrypted at rest on our servers, in cloud storage and in backups;
- passwords are stored only as encrypted hashes, and two-step verification is required for every account;
- only people who need to maintain the Service can access production systems;
- an encrypted backup is made every day and kept with a different company from the servers.

No system is 100% secure. If a security incident affects your personal data, we will tell you and the relevant authorities promptly, as the law requires.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on the law where you live (for example the GDPR in the EU and UK, PIPEDA in Canada, the CCPA/CPRA in California), you can:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
- **correct** inaccurate data (you can change your name directly in Settings → Account);
- **delete** your account and data;
- **export** your content (finished episodes can be exported in the app at any time; ask us for the rest);
- **object to or restrict** some processing, and **withdraw** consent you gave;
- **not be treated differently** for using these rights;
- **complain to your data-protection authority** (for example the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, an EU member state's authority, or the UK ICO).

To use these rights, use "Feedback" in the app or contact us as in section 13. We will reply within 30 days and may need to confirm your identity first. We do not sell or "share" (as California law defines it) your personal information.

## 12. Children

The Service is not meant for anyone under 18. If you learn that a minor has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
