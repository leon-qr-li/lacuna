R3. CONTENT TRIMS OF LEON'S OWN SPEECH (the biggest miss of v1)
======================================================================
Volume:
- 38 removed runs ≥ 2 s: about 1 per minute, 6.4 s/min.
- Lengths: p10 2.6, p25 3.0, median 4.0, p75 7.0, p90 9.8 s. Max 37.6 s.

Position within his turn (40 runs):
- Mid-turn: 13.
- Whole utterance: 12.
- Start of his turn, before he restarts cleanly: 11.
- End of his turn: 5.

Pei talks during < 20 % of these spans, usually not at all.

What they are:
1. A side anecdote or side detail that does not serve the thread.
   - Example: L002249 (757.1 s, 37.6 s), cut from "就是他他不是属于托斯卡纳地区吗…winetasting是跟一个…
     一个是biologicalengineer一个是electricityengineer…走了走了走了" through the follow-up
     L002386 "就是就一看就是这种美美国游客". The wine-tasting story with the other couples' jobs was cut.
   - Example: L001772 (614.4 s, 8.9 s), the food complaint "然后他那个面子而且意大利特别喜欢吃商面就是我亚洲我也
     不太适应啊就是没有那么熟啊就是基本上", plus L001816 "然后他们的食物确实比较plain就是我觉".
   - Example: L005370 (1973.5 s, 10.9 s), the gamer side explanation "般玩使命就是cod都是男…我死都不知道怎么死了吗他是
     打leaderboard的人啊".
2. A fumbling explanation that he then states cleanly. Keep the clean statement.
   - Example: L003956 (1453.2 s, 22.7 s), "就是scp基金会就是他那scp讲的是完了我给忘了你scp是什么是secure control
     protect好像是…我可能说的不不对啊大家纠正啊…" was CUT. "他核心设定就是…" was KEPT.
   - Example: L003578 (1187.7 s, 4.9 s), "就剧情是是符合我的预期的不是它不是符合我预期就" was cut, and "是它的剧情基本上…"
     was kept.
3. A repeated evaluation: saying the same verdict twice.
   - Example: L005518 (2008.7 s, 7.0 s), "他演的确实好吧对吧你不能说…他演的确实好啊这行活确实可以啊嗯" was cut.
     L005554 "哎呀这真的很有意思这将来…都" was cut too.
4. A trailing evaluative mumble at the end of a point.
   - Example: L003770 (1285.6 s, 9.3 s), "就是我觉得这这个有点有有有点傻这个营销方式但是啊行行吧啊就是嗯就有这种感觉嗯" was cut.
5. A redundant recap or wind-up at a topic transition.
   - Example: L002778 (954.1 s, 8.9 s), "跟大家闲聊了一下我们最近的这个新cup最近的这个出行那回到温哥华回到圣河塞之后就"
     was cut. "回顾一下我们夏天…" was kept.
6. An illustrative example that runs long.
   - Example: L004263 (1524.9 s, 12.5 s), "就是有一个就是说举个例子说一个人盯着一个电冰箱…如果你眨眼他都会把你吃了…" was cut.
7. On-air "cut this" remarks and crude asides. These are always cut.
   - Example: L000069 "反正要不管饱待会儿咱把这句话剪了吧啊".
   - Example: L000213 "那这话我得剪了…ok".
   - Example: L005176 (8.7 s) "可以这么说呀你看那些…fucking…streamer…我都吓坏了我靠啊".
   - Every "剪" remark in the half was cut (2 of 2).

KEPT in similar situations (counter-examples):
- Side detail that is short and colourful, not a sub-story: L001686 "反正那些画儿基本该看都看了".
- Audience-engagement hedges: L004900 "我不知道大家有没有意识到你看", L002991 "我不知道你的感受啊".
- Natural oral repeats inside a fluent sentence: L000520 "那个那个travel's healthy".
- The first CLEAN statement of a point is kept. Only the fumbling before it, the second restatement
  and the tail drift go.

Op guidance:
- Use cut with category "content" and confidence 0.5–0.65.
- Cut from the first token of the drift to the last token before the clean resumption.
- When Pei has no speech inside the range, or only 嗯, cut on track "both" with raw seconds.
  This keeps the tracks in sync, and Leon also lost Pei's 嗯 inside such spans.
- If Pei says something meaningful inside the range, cut around it or use keep_flag(suggested="cut").
- Aim for about 1 such trim per minute of Leon-heavy talk. Do NOT use keep_flag as a substitute when
  the pattern matches 1–7 above.

R4. TWO TRACKS: CROSSTALK, BACKCHANNELS, NOISE, RE-PLACEMENT
======================================================================
Crosstalk (153 raw overlaps ≥ 0.2 s, 82 s total):

| Outcome | Count | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Neither side cut | 127 | 83 % |
| Only Leon's side cut | 16 | 10 % |
| Only Pei's side cut | 2 | 1 % |
| Both cut | 8 | 5 % |

- When both cut, Pei restarts cleanly afterwards.
- Natural overlaps are mostly left alone. 71 of the uncut ones still overlap ≥ 0.2 s in the final.

Rule: when Leon talks over Pei with a stub, delete LEON's stub on the L track only. Leon either cut it
or muted it in place.
- L000107 "五" over Pei.
- L000126 "配" (= 呸 / Pei's name, abandoned) over Pei's "嗯不错不错".
- L002174 "而且很便宜很平民" over Pei's "哎说到这儿".
- L002197 "对他".
- L005629 "你不是哦extra" over Pei's "哦你说拆gbt".
- L001865 "对就是" over Pei's "不会吧" (637.5 s) → L-only removal.

When both stumble into each other and then restart, cut both stubs and keep the restart.
- Example (1290–1294 s): L "行/是/有这种感" and P "嗯是他需要去" were both cut. Pei's following "对他需要去…"
  was kept.

Pei's side is cut in crosstalk only when it duplicates what she then says in the clear.
- Example: P000090 "美国后花园" under Leon's "你说你去哪儿了" was muted. She answers "夏威夷" right after.

Op guidance:
- Use cut on track "L", category "crosstalk", token range, confidence 0.6. A ≤ 1 s stub does not
  meaningfully desync.
- Use a "P" cut only for an exact duplicate.

Backchannels:
- Pei: 119 short 嗯/对/哦 inside Leon's turns. 104 kept (87 %). 55 of the kept ones overlap Leon's
  words in raw, and 44 still do in the final.
- Leon's own backchannels inside Pei's turns: 18 of 19 kept.
- Leon did NOT individually re-place most backchannels.
- Their relative position changed by a median 0.4 s, almost always Pei earlier (49 earlier vs 3
  later). This is the side effect of R1's pulled-in Leon → Pei turn gaps, not a deliberate slide.

Rule:
- Keep Pei's backchannels where they are.
- Use slip (P, delta −0.3…−0.6 or +0.3…+0.8) only when a 嗯/对 collides with the middle of a Leon
  word in a way that masks it. Expect a few per 10 min, not dozens. confidence ≤ 0.4.
- Delete a Pei 嗯 only when it falls inside a Leon span you are cutting on "both".
- Never delete one on its own, and never delete a 对/是 that answers a question.

Track offset reality, for information:
- Of the final calibration half, the two tracks are in raw sync only 24 % of the time.
- For 63 %, Pei sits about 0.3–0.6 s earlier relative to Leon.
- This comes from asymmetric pause removal at turn changes. You can reproduce the listener effect
  with the R1 targets on "both" floor gaps. Do not try to reproduce the offsets themselves.
